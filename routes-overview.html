How we flyer

  • Assigned runners walk their route twice: once before the 10 Nov presentation and once before the 3 Dec presentation.
  • Free flyering: volunteers without a route carry flyers and leave them wherever they happen to go. They are listed per route with their area; contact them directly to know which shops or bars they visit.
  • Skip marks stops the assigned runner leaves out for now (greyed out below and on the map).
  • Backups: Els for any route; Alberto for Saint-Gilles, Forest and Anderlecht from November.
